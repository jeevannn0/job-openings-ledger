      What the freshness statuses mean

      ■ LIVE
      Latest refresh succeeded and its data is under 6h 30m old.
      ▲ DEGRADED/CACHED
      Latest refresh was held; cached data under 6h 30m old is shown.
      ◷ STALE
      Last successful data is older than 6h 30m.
      × FAILED
      Refresh was attempted, but no successful automated refresh is recorded yet.
      ◇ UNKNOWN/UNCHECKED
      No automated check is recorded yet (snapshot or static source).

      Data counts as fresh for 6h 30m after its last successful check; the next scheduled time is not guaranteed.
